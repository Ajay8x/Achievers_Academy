Founder & Director

Shri. Roshan Dilip Lokhande

Shri. Roshan Dilip Lokhande is the Founder and Director of Achievers Academy. With a strong passion for education and student development, he established the academy to provide quality learning, clear guidance, and a supportive academic environment.

His vision is to help students build confidence, discipline, and skills that lead to long-term success. Under his leadership, Achievers Academy focuses on excellence, innovation, and student-centered teaching.

"Education is not just about academic excellence; it's about nurturing compassionate, confident individuals who can make a positive difference in the world."
